## Grounding, publication and recovery

The app waits for successful acknowledgement, settlement and a complete assistant response. Provider errors, partial output, cancellation, malformed/undeclared artifacts and exhausted budgets cannot publish a partial Meeting. It validates artifact paths/schema/frontmatter, exact outcome citation IDs/times, Summary paragraph/list citations, literal quoted text against its cited source, and English narrative/outcomes/nested headings. Fixed outcome sections are rendered from validated extraction so separate Markdown outcome lists cannot contradict extraction. A silent Transcript deterministically yields `No speech was detected.` and no outcomes, without provider inference.

Long input is split on whole Transcript segments. Validated parts are saved atomically in the app's support state, keyed by Job identity, exact Transcript/My Notes, contract and model. Retry/restart revalidates and reuses parts; changed input or a new requested Job cannot adopt stale parts. Corrupted proposals are regenerated. A final request reconciles original evidence across parts, including later corrections and repeated commitments. Canonical artifacts remain unpublished until the final validated result succeeds. Transfer approval is checked before requests, after responses, at checkpoint writes and immediately before publication. Source changes during generation prevent stale publication. Revocation cannot retract a request already sent, but prevents subsequent requests/publication.

My Notes prose and canonical note-owned item identities remain protected; note-authored duplicates are suppressed and approved/rejected outcomes retain existing preservation behavior. Successful publication refreshes Today/Tasks. Existing workflow stage retry/order/cancellation behavior remains in force.

## Explicit supported resource envelope

- Each part contains at most 24 KiB of encoded whole Transcript segments; a larger individual segment fails without truncation. My Notes has a separate 24 KiB limit.
- At most 256 parts and eight new requests per attempt. Existing two-attempt Job policy still applies; manual Retry resumes validated progress. Final reconciliation consumes a request too.
- Every complete serialized provider prompt, including contract and Notes, must fit **128 KiB**. Every returned Summary/extraction artifact must fit 128 KiB; subprocess output has its independent 16 MiB ceiling.
- **Dense whole-Meeting evidence can exhaust the final 128 KiB context even after all parts validate.** Checkpoints do not expand the model context. This case fails with an explicit larger-context requirement; repeated Retry cannot solve it. No evidence is silently discarded to manufacture success. This is an evaluated supported-capability limit, not a claim that all recordings up to 256 parts can complete. Hierarchical synthesis of generated summaries is not used because authorized prompt evidence is restricted to Transcript/My Notes.
- Synthetic exhaustion evaluation uses eight approximately 21 KiB source segments: all eight parts checkpoint; oversized reconciliation is never sent; repeated retries reuse all parts and preserve the failure without publishing or uploading the parts again.

These are analysis resource budgets, not recording boundaries. Recordings and complete Transcript evidence remain intact on failure. Unlimited-length or dense long-recording completion, broad model correctness, and local grounded inference are not claimed.
