# Serialize complete Meeting workflows while capture runs independently

Stillmatter processes one complete Meeting workflow at a time through the reusable Jobs module, while a new recording starts immediately and existing processing continues. Separate transcription and summary schedulers sharing a heavy-work permit do not guarantee this ordering, so Meeting handlers must supply a durable staged workflow rather than relying on scheduler wake-up races. Jobs owns execution/retry/recovery and delayed not-before work; capture availability must also be separated from expensive prior-session finalization. This preserves predictable Meeting progress while allowing future non-Meeting job types without turning the current effort into a recurring-scheduling product or external daemon.
